Questions, answered

Who can join?

Any student with a university email, such as .edu or .ac.in.

How are missions matched to me?

Each mission lists the skills it needs. Your Skill Arsenal is compared against them to give every mission a match score.

What do I get for finishing a mission?

XP toward your next rank, a badge, and a case study you can print or save as a PDF.

About Project Pilot

The mission of Project Pilot is to bridge the university experience gap by connecting talented students across disciplines. While textbooks provide the theory, we provide the launchpad for real-world collaboration. Whether you are a developer, designer, or marketer, your skills are needed to bring ambitious student missions to life.
